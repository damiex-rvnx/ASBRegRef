The 1% gate controls the disposal path

The 1% line decides regulated ACM, air-district notification, and how the waste is classified. It does not decide worker protection — Cal/OSHA starts far lower, at 0.1%.

> 1%

Material is regulated asbestos-containing material (ACM) — for air-district notification and disposal classification — only when it tests greater than 1% asbestos by PLM. At or below 1% it is not regulated ACM: the friable question and the 100 ft² question trigger no air-district requirement, and the waste is not regulated asbestos waste. Cal/OSHA is a separate track — any material over 0.1% is asbestos-containing construction material (ACCM) and worker-protection rules still apply. See the caution on the ≤ 1% route.1% ACM: 40 CFR 61.141 · SCAQMD Rule 1403 · VCAPCD Rule 62.7  |  0.1% ACCM: Cal/OSHA 8 CCR 341.6, 1529

Friable vs. non-friable

▾

This split only matters above 1%. It sets the disposal path — and your demo method can move material between columns. Tap to open.
FRIABLE

Crumbles, pulverizes, or reduces to powder under hand pressure. Texture coat, joint compound, plaster, insulation.

  • Double-bag in labeled 6-mil poly asbestos bags, gooseneck / twist-and-tape each
  • Friable ≥ 1% is Title 22 hazardous waste 22 CCR Div 4.5 · DTSC
  • HW container marking — "Hazardous Waste", composition/state, hazard, generator name + address, accumulation start date, waste ID 22 CCR 66262.34(f)
  • Manifest + California LDR notification & certification to the in-state land-disposal facility 22 CCR 66268.7(a)(11)
  • Registered hazardous-waste hauler over 50 lb (self-haul ≤ 50 lb to a permitted facility, under 220 lb/mo) H&SC 25163(c)
  • Destination — Kettleman Hills, friable-permitted facility
  • Outer limit is the Title 22 accumulation window — 180 d SQG / 90 d LQG (270 d only if hauled ≥ 200 mi) 22 CCR 66262.16 / .17. Kustom SOPget to Kettleman within 10 days of removal — tighter than the rule
NON-FRIABLE

Intact, bonded material that won't release fibers under hand pressure. Some floor tile, mastic, roofing, transite.

  • Nonhazardous for disposal — non-friable isn't Title 22 hazardous waste regardless of content: no HW marking, no manifest, no registered hauler 22 CCR Div 4.5 · DTSC
  • But Cal/OSHA warning labels can still apply where the crew disturbs it — worker-exposure, not waste class 8 CCR 1529(k)
  • Keep intact — don't grind, saw, or break
  • Destination — Simi Valley Landfill, accepts non-friable ACM (call ahead)
  • Category I (packagings, gaskets, resilient floor tile, asphalt roofing) resists fiber release; Category II (transite and other cement products) can release fibers once crushed — treat crushed Cat II as friable
The flip that catches people: if your demo method renders non-friable material friable — grinding, sawing, breaking, heavy mechanical disturbance — it legally becomes friable and jumps to the stricter Kettleman path: labels, manifest, and the 10-day window all kick in. Method matters as much as material.

Three routes, three gates

Non-regulated · ≤ 1%

TRIGGER — 1% OR BELOW (air-district & disposal)
  1. Double-bag plain poly — no asbestos labels or stickers
  2. Any construction-debris landfill that accepts the material
  3. LA loads are often consolidated on a truck and hauled to Ventura for one full dump
  4. Keep the non-ACM letter with the load
  5. Check separately for lead — a TCLP may apply
⚠ Cal/OSHA still applies below 1% Over 0.1% is ACCM: registration for ≥ 100 ft², Report of Use below that, and 8 CCR 1529 work practices when disturbed. Cal/OSHA 8 CCR 1529 · 341.6–341.14

Friable · > 1%

TRIGGER — OVER 1%, FRIABLE
  1. Labeled 6-mil asbestos bags, double-bagged, gooseneck sealed
  2. Friable > 1% is Title 22 hazardous waste: HW container marking + accumulation start date on each container 22 CCR 66262.34(f) · start-date 66262.16(b)(4) / 66262.17(a)(5)
  3. Uniform hazardous-waste manifest + California LDR notification & certification with the load 22 CCR 66268.7(a)(11)
  4. Registered HW hauler over 50 lb. Generator self-haul allowed ≤ 50 lb to a permitted facility H&SC 25163(c)
  5. Kettleman Hills — friable-permitted facility
  6. 35251 Old Skyline Rd, Kettleman City 93239 · Directions ↗
  7. Kustom SOPKettleman within 10 days of removal (day 1 = removal) — tighter than the rule. Regulatory outer limit is the Title 22 accumulation window: 180 d (SQG) / 90 d (LQG); 270 d only if hauled ≥ 200 mi 22 CCR 66262.16 / .17
  8. Advance notice to air district if ≥ 100 ft² / 100 lf / 35 ft³ Rule 1403 · Rule 62.7 · cu-ft basis 40 CFR 61.145(a)

Non-friable · > 1%

TRIGGER — OVER 1%, NON-FRIABLE
  1. Nonhazardous for disposal regardless of content — no HW marking, no manifest, no registered hauler 22 CCR Div 4.5 · DTSC asbestos guidance
  2. But Cal/OSHA warning labels can still apply where the crew disturbs it — worker-exposure, not waste class 8 CCR 1529(k)
  3. Bag and keep intact — no grinding, sawing, breaking
  4. Simi Valley Landfill — accepts non-friable ACM (confirm acceptance labeling)
  5. 2801 Madera Rd, Simi Valley 93065 · call ahead / pre-approval · Directions ↗
  6. No Kustom SOPdisposal-window for non-friable
  7. Advance notice to air district if ≥ 100 ft² / 100 lf / 35 ft³ Rule 1403 · Rule 62.7 · cu-ft basis 40 CFR 61.145(a)
FIELD NOTE

Common mix-ups — two different labeling systems: Friable > 1% is Title 22 hazardous waste, so it gets HW container marking, a manifest, and a registered hauler over 50 lb. 22 CCR 66262.34(f) · 66268.7(a)(11) · H&SC 25163(c) Non-friable is nonhazardous for disposal regardless of content — no manifest, no HW marking — but Cal/OSHA warning labels can still apply where the crew disturbs it, keyed to worker exposure not waste class. 8 CCR 1529(k) Under 1% is plain debris for the air district and landfill, but over 0.1% it's still ACCM for Cal/OSHA. The 10-day-to-Kettleman clock is a Kustom SOP, tighter than the Title 22 accumulation window — confirm it against the facility.

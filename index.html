The 1% gate controls everything

Nothing else matters until you clear this. Every threshold below only activates once the material is over the line.

> 1%

Material is regulated asbestos-containing material (ACM) only when it tests greater than 1% asbestos. At or below 1% it is not regulated ACM — the friable question and the 100 ft² question trigger nothing, and the waste is not regulated asbestos waste. Cal/OSHA work practices can still be directed by your IH regardless of content.

Friable vs. non-friable

This split only matters above 1%. It sets the disposal path — and your demo method can move material between columns.

FRIABLE

Crumbles, pulverizes, or reduces to powder under hand pressure. Texture coat, joint compound, plaster, insulation.

  • Double-bag in labeled 6-mil poly asbestos bags
  • Gooseneck / twist-and-tape each bag
  • Generator info and weight labeling
  • Waste shipment record / manifest with the load
  • Destination — Kettleman Hills, friable-permitted facility
  • 10-day window — dispose within 10 days of removal (removal day = day 1)
NON-FRIABLE

Intact, bonded material that won't release fibers under hand pressure. Some floor tile, mastic, roofing, transite.

  • Still regulated ACM above 1%, but OSHA waste tracking only — no asbestos hazard labels
  • Keep intact — don't grind, saw, or break
  • Destination — Simi Valley Landfill, accepts non-friable ACM
  • No 10-day window — no disposal deadline like friable
  • Category I vs II distinctions can apply
The flip that catches people: if your demo method renders non-friable material friable — grinding, sawing, breaking, heavy mechanical disturbance — it legally becomes friable and jumps to the stricter Kettleman path: labels, manifest, and the 10-day window all kick in. Method matters as much as material.
Disposal quick-ref

Three routes, three gates

Non-regulated · ≤ 1%

TRIGGER — 1% OR BELOW
  1. Double-bag plain poly — no asbestos labels or stickers
  2. Any construction-debris landfill that accepts the material
  3. LA loads are often consolidated on a truck and hauled to Ventura for one full dump
  4. Keep the non-ACM letter with the load
  5. Check separately for lead — a TCLP may apply

Friable · > 1%

TRIGGER — OVER 1%, FRIABLE
  1. Labeled 6-mil asbestos bags, double-bagged, gooseneck sealed
  2. Generator + weight labeling, manifest / waste shipment record
  3. Kettleman Hills — friable-permitted facility
  4. 35251 Old Skyline Rd, Kettleman City 93239 · Directions ↗
  5. 10-day window — dispose within 10 days of removal (day 1 = removal day)
  6. Advance notice to air district if ≥ 100 ft²

Non-friable · > 1%

TRIGGER — OVER 1%, NON-FRIABLE
  1. OSHA waste tracking — no asbestos hazard labels
  2. Bag and keep intact — no grinding, sawing, breaking
  3. Simi Valley Landfill — accepts non-friable ACM
  4. 2801 Madera Rd, Simi Valley 93065 · call ahead / pre-approval · Directions ↗
  5. No 10-day disposal window
  6. Advance notice to air district if ≥ 100 ft²
FIELD NOTE

Common mix-ups: asbestos labels, gooseneck, and manifest are for friable material — non-friable rides on OSHA waste tracking with no hazard labels. Under 1% is plain debris — dump at any landfill that accepts it (LA loads are often trucked to Ventura and dumped once the truck's full). And friable has a clock — 10 days from removal to Kettleman.
